Our Story

Conquer Fitness was born from a simple belief: everyone deserves access to a world-class training environment. Founded in 2020, we've grown from a small garage gym into the city's most premium unisex fitness destination.

Our 6,000 sq.ft. air-conditioned facility is equipped with top-of-the-line Jerai equipment, dedicated zones for strength, cardio, CrossFit, yoga, and Zumba — plus a luxury steam bath for recovery. We believe fitness should be inclusive, motivating, and supported by expert guidance.
